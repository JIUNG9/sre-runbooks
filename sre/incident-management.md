# Incident Management

## Severity levels
| Level | Impact | Response | Escalation |
|-------|--------|----------|------------|
| SEV1 | Full outage | Immediate | VP + CTO |
| SEV2 | Degraded | 15 min | Director |
| SEV3 | Minor | 1 hour | Team lead |
| SEV4 | Cosmetic | Next sprint | None |

## Incident lifecycle
1. Detect (monitoring alert)
2. Triage (severity + owner)
3. Mitigate (stop the bleeding)
4. Resolve (root cause fix)
5. Postmortem (blameless)

## MTTR breakdown
MTTR = MTTD + MTTE + MTTF + MTTV
